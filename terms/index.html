Nutrivate is not medical advice

This is the most important thing in this document.

Nutrivate provides general nutrition and fitness information, and targets calculated from standard formulas and published nutrition guidelines (Mifflin-St Jeor for energy, and the Australian guideline of 2 serves of fruit and 5 of vegetables a day). Those calculations are estimates based on what you tell us. They know nothing about your medical history, your medications, your blood work, or your pregnancy.

Nutrivate is not a medical device, and nothing in it is a diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for advice from a qualified health practitioner.

Talk to a doctor or an accredited practising dietitian before making significant changes to your diet or exercise, and particularly if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, managing diabetes, heart or kidney disease, or have a history of disordered eating.

If you think you may be experiencing an eating disorder, the Butterfly Foundation National Helpline is 1800 33 4673.

The food data in the App comes from the FSANZ Australian Food Composition Database and from Open Food Facts, a community-maintained database. We believe it is good, but we don’t guarantee that any figure is accurate for the specific food in front of you — branded products change their recipes, and community-entered data can be wrong.
