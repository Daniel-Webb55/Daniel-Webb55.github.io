Privacy Policy

Effective 15 July 2026 · Last updated 15 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daniel Webb (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Nutrivate mobile application (the “App”). We are based in Australia and comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

By using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

A quick summary

  • Nutrivate is a nutrition and training tracker. Most of your information stays on your device by default.
  • Creating an account is optional. It exists only to back up your data to the cloud so you can restore it on a new device.
  • We collect health and dietary information (a sensitive category under Australian law) only because it is the core function you ask the App to perform — and only with your consent.
  • We do not sell your data, show third-party advertising, use analytics or tracking, or share your health data with anyone for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud data at any time from within the App.

Who we are

The App is provided by Daniel Webb, located in Australia. For any privacy question or request, contact us at coachonthewebb@gmail.com.

What information we collect

Information you enter (health and profile information). To calculate your targets and track your progress, you may provide:

  • Body details: age, gender, height, current weight, goal weight, activity level, and your chosen timeframe.
  • Nutrition logs: foods and meals you record (including calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fibre, sugars, and micronutrients), water intake, saved recipes, and meal photos.
  • Training logs: exercise sessions, sets, reps, weights, distances, and durations.
  • Body measurements: weight and body measurements you log over time (e.g. waist, chest, hips, arm, thigh).
  • Tracking preferences: which of the above you choose to track.

Much of this is health information and therefore sensitive information under the Privacy Act. We collect it only with your consent and only to provide the App’s features.

Account information (only if you create an account). If you choose to create an account for cloud backup, we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are stored by them in hashed form; we never see or store your plain-text password.

Information collected automatically. The App itself does not include third-party analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage-tracking tools. Standard technical information (such as your device’s IP address) may be processed by our infrastructure provider when your device connects to our servers, solely to deliver and secure the service.

How we use your information

  • Calculate your nutrition and training targets and show your progress.
  • Save your data on your device and, if you have an account, back it up so you can restore it on another device.
  • Look up branded foods and barcodes you search for (see “Third parties” below).
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Respond to your support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties, and we do not use your health information for any purpose other than providing the features you have chosen to use.

Where your information is stored and how we protect it

  • On your device: Your logs and settings are stored locally on your device. Meal photos you attach are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • In the cloud (only with an account): If you create an account, your tracked data (excluding meal photos) is backed up to our database hosted by Supabase in the Sydney, Australia region. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Access controls: Every record is protected by row-level security so that your account can only ever access your own data. Your login session on the device is stored in the device’s secure keychain.

While no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, we take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access, in line with APP 11.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law (for example, in response to a valid legal request).

How long we keep your information

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Cloud data (if you have an account) is kept until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete your account, your account and its associated cloud data are permanently removed from our database.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the App without an account — keeping all data on your device.
  • Access and correct your information directly in the App at any time.
  • Delete your account and cloud data from within the App (Settings → Account → Delete account). This permanently erases your cloud data; data on your device is removed by deleting the App or clearing its data.
  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting coachonthewebb@gmail.com.

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first. If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Overseas storage

Your cloud data is stored in Australia (Supabase, Sydney region). Requests to Open Food Facts are served from their infrastructure, which may be located overseas; those requests contain only your search term or barcode, not your personal or health information.

Not medical advice

Nutrivate provides general nutrition and fitness information and calculated targets based on general references. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional advice from a qualified health practitioner. Always consult a professional before making significant changes to your diet or exercise.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Your continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
