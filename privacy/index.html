Privacy Policy

Effective 15 July 2026 · Last updated 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daniel Webb (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Nutrivate mobile application (the “App”). We are based in Australia and comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

This Policy is a notice explaining how we handle your information. You consent to specific things inside the App — the health-data tick, the “Allow email marketing, news and offers” switch when you create an account, and the progress-photo backup switch — and each of those is a separate choice. Marketing emails are stopped with the unsubscribe link or by emailing us (see “What information we collect” below).

A quick summary

  • Nutrivate is a nutrition and training tracker. Most of your information stays on your device by default.
  • Creating an account is optional. It is used to back up your data to the cloud so you can restore it on a new device. When you create one, you are also asked whether to allow email marketing, which is off unless you switch it on.
  • We collect health and dietary information (a sensitive category under Australian law) only because it is the core function you ask the App to perform — and only with your consent.
  • Voice logging happens entirely on your device. Your speech is converted to text by iOS itself — no recording is sent to us or to anyone else, and nothing is kept except the food entry you confirm.
  • The Cookbook is the one feature that saves audio, and only if you ask it to. It is switched off until you turn it on. If you record someone reading out a recipe step, that recording is saved on your device and is not uploaded to us.
  • Check Ins are your own daily habits — you choose what to track and what to call it. They are stored like the rest of your data: on your device, and in your cloud backup if you have an account. We treat them as health information.
  • Progress photos are optional, and stay on your phone unless you switch on backup. You can add photos of yourself in Check Ins. Backing them up to the cloud is a separate switch that is off until you turn it on, and it needs an account. They are photos of your body, so we treat them as health information.
  • Email marketing is your choice, and off until you say yes. When you create an account, there is one switch: “Allow email marketing, news and offers”. It is off by default. If you switch it on, Daniel may email you about Nutrivate coaching, news and offers, using only your name and email — never your food, health or body data. There is no switch in the App afterwards: to stop, use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or email us, and it is actioned as soon as we receive it, and always within 5 working days.
  • We do not sell your data, show third-party advertising, use analytics or tracking, or share your health data with anyone for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud data at any time from within the App.

Who we are

The App is provided by Daniel Webb, located in Australia. For any privacy question or request, contact us at coachonthewebb@gmail.com.

What information we collect

Information you enter (health and profile information). To calculate your targets and track your progress, you may provide:

  • Your name: a first and last name, so the App can greet you by name and, only if you switch on email marketing, address you in marketing emails. It is optional — leave it out and the App simply says “G’day”. You can change or clear it at any time from Edit my details.
  • Body details: age, gender, height, current weight, goal weight, activity level, and your chosen timeframe.
  • Nutrition logs: foods and meals you record (including calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fibre, sugars, and micronutrients), water intake, saved recipes, and meal photos.
  • Training logs: exercise sessions, sets, reps, weights, distances, and durations.
  • Body measurements: weight and body measurements you log over time (e.g. waist, chest, hips, arm, thigh).
  • Check Ins (only if you add some): daily habits you make for yourself. We keep the name you give each habit, an optional unit (such as “glasses”), and what you log against it each day — a tick, a number you type, or a rating from 1 to 5. Because you choose the names, a Check In can say something about your health (for example a medication, a symptom, or how you slept), and we treat all of it as health information. Please do not put anyone else’s name or details in a habit name.
  • Progress photos (only if you add some): photos of yourself that you take with the camera or choose from your photo library, each filed under a day. They are always saved on your phone. See “Progress photos and optional backup” below. Because they are photos of your body, we treat them as health information.
  • Cookbook dishes (only if you turn the Cookbook on): dish names, the steps you write or dictate, ingredients, photos of a handwritten recipe card, and any voice recordings you choose to save.
  • Tracking preferences: which of the above you choose to track.

Much of this is health information and therefore sensitive information under the Privacy Act. We collect it only with your consent and only to provide the App’s features.

Account information (only if you create an account). If you choose to create an account for cloud backup, we collect your name, email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are stored by them in hashed form; we never see or store your plain-text password.

Email marketing is opt-in, and the switch starts off. When you create an account in the App, there is one switch: “Allow email marketing, news and offers”. It is off by default. If you switch it on, you agree that Daniel may email you about Nutrivate coaching, news and offers, including following up with you if you show interest in coaching. These are Daniel’s own services only. We never send third-party offers, and we never sell or share your details for marketing. For this we use only your name and email address. Your food, health and body data are never used to choose who we contact or what we say. The switch is part of creating an account, because that is where your email address comes from.

We keep a record of when you agreed or withdrew, and which wording you agreed to, on our server in Australia. If you switch it on, your choice is held on your phone until you confirm your email address, and is recorded on our server the first time you sign in on that same phone, within 14 days of creating your account. If you first sign in on a different phone, or more than 14 days later, it is not recorded and we will not send you marketing emails; email us if you would like them. Withdrawals you send us by email or by the unsubscribe link are added to the record by us. If you delete your account, this record is deleted with it. You can withdraw your consent at any time. There is no switch for it in the App after sign-up. To stop, use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or email us at coachonthewebb@gmail.com. We action it as soon as we receive it, and always within 5 working days. Every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link. Account emails, such as confirming your address or resetting your password, are not marketing and do not carry one. If you did not switch it on when you created your account and later want marketing emails, you can email us to ask. This consent is separate from using the App: you can use every feature, with or without an account, without agreeing to it. If you do not agree, we only ever email you about your account.

Progress photos and optional backup. Progress photos are optional. You add them in Check Ins (Profile → Check Ins), by taking a photo with the camera or choosing one from your photo library, and you file each one under a day. Only add photos of yourself, and please do not back up nude or sexually explicit photos. They are photos of your body, which makes them health information and sensitive information under the Privacy Act, so they are handled under the same consent and security as the rest of your health information.

On your phone. Every progress photo is always saved on your phone. The App asks iOS to re-save each photo as a JPEG, which normally removes location data, and checks every photo before it is uploaded. A photo that still carries location details stays on your phone and is not uploaded. There are limits of 10 photos a day and 200 in total.

Cloud backup is a separate switch, and it is off until you turn it on. It needs an account. Switching it on opens its own consent sheet that explains what will happen, and it only turns on once you agree and our server has recorded your agreement. On our server in Australia we record that you agreed, the time, which wording you agreed to, and a short history of any changes. This consent is separate from the health-data tick and from using the App: every other feature works the same without it.

What is uploaded. Only the photo itself, the day you filed it under, and an id for it (our server also notes when it was uploaded). When you switch backup on, that includes photos already on your phone that you added while signed in to this account. Photos you added before you signed in are only backed up if you tick a separate box in that consent sheet. A photo is not uploaded, and stays on your phone only, if it is over about 6 MB, if it still carries location details, or if it would take you over the limit of 200 photos in the cloud.

Where they are kept and who can see them. Backed-up photos are stored privately in Supabase Storage in Sydney, Australia. Nothing is public, and no other user of the App can see them. Supabase stores them for us. As the operator we can technically access stored data to run the service, but we do not look at your photos unless you ask us to or the law requires it. The App shows them to you through short-lived links that expire within minutes, and saves one onto a phone only if you choose to. We do not share your progress photos with coaches or anyone else. If we ever offered to share them, for example with a coach, we would ask for your separate consent first.

You can delete a photo, switch backup off, or delete your account at any time: see “How long we keep your information” and “Your choices and rights” below.

Voice, microphone and camera

These are used only while you are actively using the feature that needs them, and iOS asks for your permission the first time where it requires it.

  • Voice logging (microphone + speech recognition). When you tap the microphone and speak, your device’s own built-in speech recognition (Apple’s Speech framework) converts what you say into text so the App can find the food you named. The processing is performed on your device by iOS. When you log food this way Nutrivate does not record audio, does not store audio, and does not send any audio or transcript to our servers or to any third party. (Saving a recording is only possible in the Cookbook, described next, and only when you ask for it.) The only thing kept is the food entry you confirm — exactly as if you had typed it. Speech recognition is handled by Apple as part of iOS and is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.
  • Cookbook recordings (optional, and off by default). The Cookbook lets you save a dish as spoken steps, so you can keep a family recipe in the voice of the person who taught it to you. This is the one place the App does keep audio. When you choose to record a step, the recording is saved on your device; it is not uploaded to our servers and is not included in cloud backup. Deleting the step or the dish deletes the recording with it.
  • Recording someone other than yourself. If the voice you are saving is not your own, the App asks you to confirm that person has agreed, by name, before it records — and offers to simply write their words down instead. Please do not record anyone who has not agreed to it. A person’s voice is their personal information as much as yours is.
  • Camera. Used to scan barcodes, to photograph meals and to take progress photos. Barcode scanning sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts.
  • Photo library. Used only when you choose a photo of a meal or of your progress from your library. You pick the photo in iOS’s own picker, and the App receives only the photo you choose.
  • Meal photos. Photos you attach to a meal are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers, and are not included in cloud backup. Progress photos are different: see “Progress photos and optional backup” above.

Information collected automatically. The App itself does not include third-party analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage-tracking tools. Standard technical information (such as your device’s IP address) may be processed by our infrastructure provider when your device connects to our servers, solely to deliver and secure the service.

How we use your information

  • Calculate your nutrition and training targets and show your progress.
  • Save your data on your device and, if you have an account, back it up so you can restore it on another device.
  • Look up branded foods and barcodes you search for (see “Third parties” below).
  • Show your Check Ins and the history of what you logged against them.
  • Show your progress photos and, only if you switch on photo backup, keep a private copy in the cloud so you can get them back on another phone. We use them for nothing else.
  • Turn speech into text on your device when you log food by voice.
  • Greet you by name, if you gave us one.
  • Email you about Nutrivate coaching, news and offers, and follow up if you show interest in coaching, only if you switched on “Allow email marketing, news and offers” when you created your account. We use only your name and email for this, never your food, health or body data, and we stop when you withdraw consent (see “What information we collect” above for how).
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Respond to your support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties, and we do not use your health information for any purpose other than providing the features you have chosen to use.

Where your information is stored and how we protect it

  • On your device: Your logs and settings are stored locally on your device. Meal photos you attach, Cookbook voice recordings, and photos of handwritten recipe cards are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Progress photos are also always saved on your device; they are uploaded only if you switch on photo backup.
  • In the cloud (only with an account): If you create an account, your tracked data is backed up to our database hosted by Supabase in the Sydney, Australia region. This includes the written part of a Cookbook dish — its name, steps and ingredients — so you do not lose it if you change phones. It also includes your Check Ins: the habit names and units you created, and the daily ticks, numbers and ratings you logged. It excludes meal photos, voice recordings and recipe-card photos, which stay on the device, and it includes progress photos only if you switch on photo backup (next point). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Progress photo backup (only if you switch it on): the photos are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket in the Sydney, Australia region, in a private folder that the App’s access rules limit to your own account, so no other user can see it, with a small index in our database (each photo’s id, its day, and when it was uploaded). Nothing is public. Our database refuses an upload unless the consent record on our server says backup is on, and it limits each person to 200 photos of up to about 6 MB each. Like the rest of your cloud data, they are encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Access controls: Every record is protected by row-level security so that your account can only ever access your own data. Photo storage has its own rules of the same kind, so no other user can reach your folder. Your login session on the device is stored in the device’s secure keychain.

While no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, we take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access, in line with APP 11.

How long we keep your information

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Cloud data (if you have an account) is kept until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete your account, your account and its associated cloud data are deleted from our database and photo files are deleted from storage. Deleted database records may remain for a limited time in our host’s encrypted backups until those backups expire.
  • Progress photos on your phone stay until you delete them, delete the App or clear its data.
  • Progress photos in the cloud are kept until you delete them, switch backup off and choose to delete the cloud copies, or delete your account. Deleting a photo in the App deletes it from your phone straight away, and deletes its cloud copy the next time Nutrivate is open on this phone, online and signed in to that account (the App retries only while it is open, and the list of deletions waiting is kept on that phone only). If you remove the App first, ask us at coachonthewebb@gmail.com and we will delete the cloud copy, or sign in again and remove the photo from the list of backed-up photos in Check Ins. When you switch backup off, the App asks whether to delete your cloud copies (the default, and the recommended choice) or keep them; kept copies stay private to you and stay visible, and deletable, in the App. When you delete your account, the App first deletes all your cloud photos, and if they cannot be removed the account is not deleted. If the account deletion itself then fails after the photos have been removed, your cloud photos may already be gone and photo backup will be switched off, and the App tells you so.
  • Removed Check Ins. When you remove a Check In, the App hides it and stops asking you about it, but keeps what you already logged so your past days still make sense. It is not erased until you delete your account (which erases it from the cloud) or delete the App or clear its data (which erases it from your device). If you want a habit’s name gone sooner, ask us at coachonthewebb@gmail.com.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the App without an account — keeping all data on your device.
  • Access and correct your information directly in the App at any time.
  • Delete your account and cloud data from within the App (Profile → Settings → Account & cloud backup → Delete account). This erases your cloud data (see “How long we keep your information” for how long copies can linger in our host’s backups), including all your backed-up progress photos; data on your device is removed by deleting the App or clearing its data.
  • Delete a progress photo, or switch photo backup on or off, in Profile → Check Ins → Progress photos. Switching backup off withdraws your consent, which is recorded on our server, and lets you choose whether to delete the cloud copies.
  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting coachonthewebb@gmail.com.

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first. If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

Children’s privacy

Nutrivate is for people aged 16 and over, and our Terms of Use say so. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone younger has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

The App itself contains nothing unsuitable for a younger person, which is why its App Store rating is 4+. The age limit is not about content — it is because the App handles health information, and that deserves someone old enough to decide for themselves whether to share it.

Overseas storage

Your cloud data is stored in Australia (Supabase, Sydney region). Requests to Open Food Facts are served from their infrastructure, which may be located overseas; those requests contain only your search term or barcode, not your personal or health information.

It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional advice from a qualified health practitioner. Always consult a professional before making significant changes to your diet or exercise.
